Mini Weather Station

A low-cost, battery-powered weather station built on an ESP32. It measures rain, temperature, humidity, pressure, light and wind, and sends one reading every 10 minutes over LoRaWAN. Several identical copies will be compared against the CIHEAM Bari reference station.

Interval 10 min Radio LoRaWAN EU868 Message 26 bytes Power 2 × 18650

How it spends its time

The station is asleep almost all the time. It wakes on the 10-minute mark to measure and transmit, and for a fraction of a second whenever the rain gauge tips.

What it measures

CH 1

Rainfall

mm · 0.2 mm per tip

MB-MET 200 tipping-bucket gauge. Totals kept for 10 min, day, month and all time.

CH 2

Air temperature & humidity

°C · %RH

SHT3x digital sensor (I²C).

CH 3

Pressure

hPa

BMP280. Also a backup temperature if the SHT3x fails.

CH 4

Light → solar radiation

lux → W/m²

BH1750 under a dome. Converted to radiation with 0.0079 W/m² per lux.

CH 5

Wind speed & gust

m/s

12 V analogue anemometer (0.4–2 V). 10-second sample; gust is the highest 1-second mean.

CH 6

Link quality

dBm · dB

Signal strength, SNR and number of gateways that heard each message.

From sensor to dashboard

  1. ESP32 + RFM95W radio. Reads the sensors and packs everything into a 26-byte LoRaWAN message.
  2. The Things Network (TTN). A nearby gateway receives it; TTN decodes the bytes into named values.
  3. Ubidots. A webhook forwards the values. Per-station calibration is applied there, then the data is stored and plotted.
  4. Comparison. Readings are compared hour by hour with the CIHEAM Bari reference station to calibrate each replica.
